Live demo of [support-inbox](https://github.com/vaishsRP/support-inbox): write to a
made-up student housing company, then switch to the support team's side to see the
draft the assistant prepared, what it refused, what it blocked, and what landed on
the action list. The company and its emails are invented. Nothing is ever sent.
