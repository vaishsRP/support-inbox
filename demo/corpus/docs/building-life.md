# Living in the building

Internet: every room has wifi (network HB-Residents) and a wired port. Log in with your tenant number and the password from your welcome email. If the connection drops, restart the device first; if the whole floor is down, report it through the portal.

Post and parcels: post is delivered to the letterboxes in the entrance hall, labelled with your room number. Parcels too large for the letterbox are held at the office for 14 days; bring ID.

Bikes: park bikes only in the bike cellar, entrance at the back of the building. Bikes left in the hallways or on the pavement in front of the entrance are removed after a warning label. Abandoned bikes are cleared twice a year.

Noise: quiet hours are 23:00 to 07:00. For noise complaints, first talk to your neighbour if you feel comfortable. If it keeps happening, report it through the portal with dates and times; the house manager follows up.

Cleaning: tenants on each floor share cleaning of the kitchen on a rota. The common hallways and stairs are cleaned weekly by a cleaning company.
