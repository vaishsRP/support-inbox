# Repairs and maintenance

Report repairs through the tenant portal (portal.harbourbrook.example) with a photo and a short description.

Urgent repairs are handled the same day. Urgent means: no heating between October and April, no hot water, a water leak, no electricity in your room, a lock that does not work or a broken outside door. For urgent problems outside office hours, call the emergency line on +31 20 555 0199.

Non-urgent repairs (a dripping tap, a broken shelf, a lamp) are scheduled within 5 working days. The caretaker may enter your room for a scheduled repair after notice by email, also when you are not at home.

Washing machines and dryers in the laundry room are maintained by an outside company. Report a broken machine with the number on its front.

Damage caused by a tenant is repaired at the tenant's cost.
