# Contracts, rent and registration

Contracts run for a minimum of 5 months. The notice period is one calendar month, given by email, ending on the last day of a month.

Leaving before the end of the minimum period is only possible if an approved replacement tenant takes over the contract. You may suggest someone; the office checks and approves them.

Subletting your room, or letting someone else live in it, is not allowed. Guests may stay a maximum of 3 nights in a row and 10 nights per month.

Rent is due on the 1st of each month. A reminder is sent on the 5th. From the 8th a late fee of EUR 15 is added. If you cannot pay on time, contact the finance team before the 1st: only finance can agree a payment plan.

Registration letter: to register your address at the Westmere town hall you need a letter from us confirming your tenancy. Request it through the portal after you have collected your keys; we send it within 3 working days.

Contract extensions are offered 2 months before the end date, subject to availability. Room changes within Harbourbrook are possible once per contract, for an admin fee of EUR 50, if a room is free.
