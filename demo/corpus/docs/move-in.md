# Moving in and keys

Keys are collected at the Harbourbrook office, Kadeweg 12, Westmere, Monday to Friday between 10:00 and 16:00.

Before we can hand over keys we need three things: your signed contract, your first month's rent and your deposit, both received on our account.

Arriving outside office hours, at weekends or on a public holiday? Tell us your arrival date at least 3 working days in advance. We then send you a code for the key box at the building entrance 24 hours before you arrive. Key box pick-up is free.

Late arrival on the same day: if your flight or train is delayed, email us. If the office has closed, the key box can be arranged only if the code was requested in advance.

Your room is inspected before you arrive. Report anything that is damaged or missing within 7 days of moving in, with photos, so it is not charged to you when you leave.
