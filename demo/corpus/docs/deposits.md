# Deposits and refunds

Every tenant pays a deposit of one month's rent before moving in. It is held until the tenancy ends.

After you move out, our caretaker inspects the room within 5 working days. The deposit is paid back to the bank account you gave us within 30 days of your move-out date, minus any deductions for damage, missing items or unpaid rent. You receive an itemised statement of any deductions by email.

Normal wear and tear is never deducted. Typical deductions: a deep clean if the room is left dirty (EUR 85), a replacement key or key card (EUR 45), wall damage and holes (from EUR 60), missing furniture (replacement cost).

To dispute a deduction, reply to the statement within 14 days with photos. Disputes are reviewed by the finance team, not by the support desk.

Only the finance team can approve a refund, an early refund or a waived deduction. Support staff cannot promise amounts or dates beyond this policy.
