# Demo company: Harbourbrook Student Homes (made up)

**Everything in this folder is invented for the demo.** Harbourbrook Student
Homes, the city of Westmere, the people, the policies and every email were written
for this project. Any resemblance to a real company is unintended.

It exists because the live demo needs a company whose past emails and policies
visitors can see, and the real evaluation data (public tweets to real brands)
cannot be dressed up as someone else. The honest numbers about whether the
approach works come from that real data, in `reports/`, not from this folder.

- `pairs.yaml`: about 100 past customer emails and the replies the team sent,
  in the helpdesk-export shape the importer reads. Written to look like a real
  team's history: messy questions, a consistent house voice, repeat questions,
  some Dutch and German.
- `docs/`: the policy pages a support manager would upload on the context page.
