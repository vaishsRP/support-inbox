A made-up company, for this demo only
How this works. On the Customer side you write to a student housing company. On the Support team side you see what their assistant did with it: a draft adapted from a past reply, a draft built from their policy pages, a refusal with a reason, or a block when a person must handle it. Anything that would promise money, a deadline or an exception is left as a red placeholder for someone with authority. Nothing is ever sent by the assistant: a person always presses send. In real use this all happens inside Gmail; this page imitates it.
